### [claude-trading-skills](https://github.com/tradermonty/claude-trading-skills)
`status: watching` · `tags: agent-skill, claude-code, trading, risk, journaling, workflow` · added 2026-11

A Claude Skills toolkit by TraderMonty for equity investors and traders: market analysis, charting, screeners, risk checks, trade planning, and journaling. The official README says the goal is not to outsource buy/sell decisions to AI, but to structure market review, risk management, trade planning, journaling, and continuous improvement.
**Why I care:** The standout is the risk-discipline layer — pre-trade checks, drawdown stops, position sizing, and a trade journal — rather than another stock picker.
**Notes:** MIT (Copyright 2026 TraderMonty). 74 skills under `skills/` and 3,811 stars as of 2026-11. Highlighted skills confirmed in the repo: `pre-trade-discipline-gate`, `drawdown-circuit-breaker`, `position-sizer`, `trader-memory-core`. The project's README states it is not financial advice or a signal service. Discovered via [工程師阿穆 | Build with AI (@homuchen.build.ai)](https://www.threads.com/@homuchen.build.ai/post/Dfo5Qmmk8tG); the post cited 3,663 stars. [Author continuation](https://www.threads.com/@homuchen.build.ai/post/Dfo5RnDky1M) says the data sources are US-stock only (not independently verified as a hard repo-wide limit). His [writeup](https://homuchen.com/posts/claude-stock-skill-tradermonty-taiwan/) describes adapting `market-breadth-analyzer` for Taiwan stocks with FinMind, published as [HoMuChen/tw-market-breadth-skill](https://github.com/HoMuChen/tw-market-breadth-skill).
