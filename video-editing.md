### [Prompt Motion](https://prompt-motion.com/)
`status: watching` · `tags: inspiration, prompts, agent-skill, motion-graphics, ai-video` · added 2026-10

A gallery of motion videos made with Claude Opus 5.5, each shown with the prompt or skill that produced it. Filter by style: product UI, phone, charts, diagrams, kinetic type, shapes, particles, characters, photos, music, and code.
**Why I care:** A reference library to start from. Find a video close to the look I want, copy its prompt or skill, and adapt it to my own product. Pairs well with [cinematic-shot](#cinematic-shot) for launch videos.
**Notes:** The site lets you customize a prompt for your own project, and has a weekly email recap. Made by [@p4nthera_](https://x.com/p4nthera_).

### [cinematic-shot](https://github.com/1weiho/cinematic-shot)
`status: watching` · `tags: agent-skill, launch-video, product-marketing, stills` · added 2026-10

An agent skill that directs cinematic product launch films: steep, close-up "hero shots" of real UI floating on a dark or studio stage, with shallow depth of field and a slow camera drift, cut together and ending on one line of copy and the logo. It can also shoot matching stills (hero image, social card, poster).
**Why I care:** It gives an AI agent a full art direction (story, shot list, light, motion, sound) for polished launch videos of my own products without hand-editing. It doesn't pick the rendering stack, so it should work with whatever tool I render with.
**Notes:** Install with `npx skills add 1weiho/cinematic-shot`, then ask the agent something like "Make a cinematic launch video for our new search feature." The method lives in `SKILL.md`, the exact recipes in `references/look.md`, and a sample 52 s shot list in `references/shot-list-example.md`. MIT licensed.
